# GlacierNET-KZ v0.4.0

This release turns the project's separate ML, physical-context, and
decision-support evidence into one auditable workflow without collapsing them
into a misleading composite score.

## Highlights

- CentralAsia-GlacierBench publishes five measured model evaluations and four
  independent reference-evidence tracks with source states, artifact hashes,
  evidence tiers, scope, and claim boundaries.
- The strongest current local multimodal track reports Dice 0.9036 and IoU
  0.8242 on the stated one-AOI silver-label ablation. This is not independent
  expert-label accuracy.
- The Active Evidence Acquisition track is intentionally blocked until
  primary-source-reviewed events, verified controls, immutable pre-event
  replays, and realised observation-value rows exist.
- Risk Twin follows real HydroRIVERS `NEXT_DOWN` topology, draws a
  750-metre planning corridor, and surfaces public OpenStreetMap objects for
  verification. It is not a hydrodynamic flood model or warning system.
- The product hub now presents one primary path: ML evidence, Risk Twin,
  Operations, then Benchmark.
