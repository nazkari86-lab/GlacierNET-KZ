# Competition Scope

## Primary research question

Does adding Sentinel-1 VV/VH to a compact Sentinel-2 plus terrain U-Net improve
2024 glacier segmentation and area estimation on a leakage-resistant temporal
holdout in the Ile Alatau?

This is the project's strongest current, reproducible contribution. The answer
on the one-AOI silver benchmark is yes for hard Dice, hard IoU, recall, and
absolute area error under validation-calibrated thresholds.

## Product extension

The Active Cryosphere Risk Twin is a decision-oriented research baseline built
around provenance, abstention, cascade coverage, and Value of Information. It
is a useful downstream application of glacier evidence, but it is not presented
as a calibrated hazard-warning system.

## Claim boundary

Cross-region accuracy, gold-label accuracy, field validity, calibrated event
probability, operational warning performance, and causal resilience effects
remain external validation targets. They become eligible claims only when the
strict benchmark gates pass with source-reviewed evidence.
